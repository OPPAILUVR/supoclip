## 2. Root Cause: Why Did It Cut at 19 Seconds?

1. **AI "Loop Rate" Bias**:
   - The AI moment detection model returned candidate timestamps `03:34 -> 03:53` with the internal justification:
     > *"His polarizing verdict on Twitch culture sparks heavy comment debate, while the tight 19-second runtime maximizes completion and loop rate."*
   - The AI artificially squeezed the clip to 19s to optimize for short loop metrics, completely ignoring that the speaker was actively mid-sentence.
2. **Missing Semantic Boundary Verification**:
   - The previous system only checked numerical audio segment endpoints without analyzing the grammatical or semantic completeness of the sentence.
   - It treated `19.04s` as a valid boundary simply because Whisper produced a timestamp there, even though the text was `"And let me."`.
3. **Absence of Backtrack / Forward-Expansion Logic**:
   - When the speaker opened a new explanatory clause, the clipper lacked forward-scanning to capture the explanation, and lacked backtrack logic to safely cut after the completed thought (*"Not even in a million years, man."*).

---

## 3. The Cutting Skill: Where to Cut and Where NOT to Cut

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A["Raw Moment (Catalyst + Peak Reaction)"] --> B{"Current Duration < 35s OR Ending Dangling?"}
    B -- "Yes" --> C["Scan Forward to Next Whisper Segments"]
    C --> D{"Does full explanation fit within 58.0s?"}
    D -- "Yes (Normal Case)" --> E["Include Full Explanation + 0.8s Audio Decay<br/>Result: 35s - 50s High-Value Short"]
    D -- "No (Runaway Thought > 58s)" --> F["Intelligent Backtrack to Last Terminal Sentence<br/>Cut cleanly after punchline (. ! ?)"]
    B -- "No (Already complete & > 35s)" --> G["Add 0.8s Natural Pause Decay<br/>Clean Cut"]
    F --> H["Final Output: 100% Complete Narrative"]
    E --> H
    G --> H
```

### ❌ WHERE NOT TO CUT (Strictly Forbidden)
1. **Dangling Connectives & Conjunctions**:
   - Never cut on: `and`, `but`, `or`, `nor`, `because`, `cause`, `since`, `as`, `although`, `though`, `while`, `whereas`, `if`, `unless`, `until`, `so`.
2. **Introductory / Setup Clauses**:
   - Never cut on: `"And let me..."`, `"Let me tell you..."`, `"The reason why..."`, `"Here is why..."`, `"What happens is..."`, `"Watch this..."`, `"Here it comes..."`, `"Why I wouldn't..."`.
3. **Mid-Explanation or Pre-Payoff**:
   - If a creator answers a premise and begins explaining *why* they took that stance, cutting before the explanation finishes leaves the audience frustrated and ruins watch time.
4. **Micro-Duration Cuts (< 25s)**:
   - Squeezing clips under 25 seconds for loop rate when context or resolution is missing creates low-quality, unusable clips.

### ✅ WHERE TO CUT (The Perfect Out-Point)
1. **Grammatically Terminal Boundary**:
   - The sentence must conclude on a period (`.`), exclamation mark (`!`), or question mark (`?`) where both subject and predicate have resolved.
2. **Natural Silence & Audio Decay**:
   - Add `0.8s - 1.2s` of post-roll after the final word so vocal resonance, laughter decay, or the streamer's reaction face lands organically without clipping audio phonemes.
3. **Target Sweet Spot (35s – 55s, Max 58.0s)**:
   - Always encompass the full 3-act structure:
     - **Setup / Catalyst (0–15s)**: Chat prompt, external video clip, or gameplay scenario.
     - **Climax / Reaction (15–35s)**: Outburst, laugh, denial, or shock.
     - **Resolution / Explanation (35–50s)**: The breakdown of why it happened, final verdict, or comedic punchline.

### A. Semantic Boundary Guard (`is_phrase_incomplete`)

INVESTIGATION 10: YOUTUBE LIVE HLS DUAL-TRACK AUDIO/VIDEO SEPARATION ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
Unlike Twitch and Kick (which stream a single multiplexed MP4/TS HLS transport container with both video and audio tracks combined), YouTube Live separates audio and video into distinct HLS manifest streams (e.g. video on itag 270/312 and audio on itag 234).

ROOT CAUSE IDENTIFIED:
When yt-dlp probed a YouTube live stream, the default extracted URL was often video-only (vcodec: avc1, acodec: none). Capturing with a single '-i stream_url' command produced raw MP4 segments with 0 audio channels. When the automated Whisper pipeline processed these segments, ffprobe detected no audio stream and Whisper transcription aborted with 0 clips generated (causing 0 clips for IShowSpeed and Kai Cenat).

TECHNICAL RESOLUTION:
1. Updated LiveStreamService.probe_live_status(): When platform is YouTube (or URL contains youtube.com/youtu.be), yt-dlp formats are parsed in reverse order to extract BOTH:
   - Best video manifest URL (1080p/720p with vcodec != 'none')
   - Best audio manifest URL (AAC 48kHz stereo with resolution == 'audio only' or acodec != 'none')
2. Synchronized Dual-Stream FFmpeg Ingest:
   ffmpeg -y \
     -reconnect 1 -reconnect_streamed 1 -reconnect_delay_max 5 -i <video_manifest> \
     -reconnect 1 -reconnect_streamed 1 -reconnect_delay_max 5 -i <audio_manifest> \
     -map 0:v:0 -map 1:a:0 \
     -c:v copy -c:a aac \
     -f segment -segment_time 60 -reset_timestamps 1 chunk_%04d.mp4
3. Explicit Mapping Guard: '-map 0:v:0 -map 1:a:0' prevents FFmpeg default stream selection from ignoring input 1.
4. Permanent Default Setting: Automatic YouTube detection in normalize_streamer_url() and add_streamer() makes this the permanent unbypassable default for all current and future YouTube streamers.

INVESTIGATION 11: YOUTUBE STUDIO HEADLESS DOM REVERSE ENGINEERING & DESCRIPTION LOCATOR
================================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
Automated Playwright uploading on YouTube Studio was failing to fill the description box, leaving descriptions completely blank on published Shorts.

ROOT CAUSE IDENTIFIED:
The previous selector in youtube_headless_uploader.py was:
page.locator("#textbox[aria-label*='description'], ytcp-mention-textbox[label*='Description'] #textbox").first

In the actual YouTube Studio DOM, the description container is:
<ytcp-social-suggestions-textbox id="description-textarea">
    <div id="textbox" aria-label="Tell viewers about your video (type @ to mention a channel)"></div>
</ytcp-social-suggestions-textbox>

Because YouTube does not put the word 'description' in the aria-label attribute, the previous locator evaluated to count() == 0, silently skipping the description fill.

TECHNICAL RESOLUTION:
1. Updated DOM locator to:
   desc_box = page.locator("#description-textarea #textbox, [aria-label*='Tell viewers' i]").first
2. Switched from slow keyboard typing to direct .fill(description) which properly triggers YouTube's Polymer/Lit reactivity.
3. Added a hard abort assertion: If the description box locator fails, the uploader raises a RuntimeError('CHECKLIST VIOLATION: Description is MANDATORY') and auto-discards the dialog, preventing any video from uploading without a description.
4. Batch Description Retrofit: Built and executed scripts/retrofit_all_descriptions.py which navigated through all 79 existing channel videos on YouTube Studio and populated rich descriptions with streamer credits, original links, Fair Use disclaimers, and viral hashtags.

INVESTIGATION 12: YOUTUBE 24-HOUR DAILY QUOTAS & PLATFORM CEILINGS ON VERIFIED CHANNELS
================================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
Even on fully verified YouTube accounts with Intermediate (phone verification) and Advanced features enabled, automated batch uploading hit daily quota locks after publishing over 80+ Shorts in a single day.

ROOT CAUSE IDENTIFIED:
YouTube enforces two distinct rolling 24-hour ceilings:
1. Daily Upload Cap: Between 50 to 100 uploads per rolling 24 hours across the platform to mitigate automated flooding. When exceeded, YouTube Studio displays: 'Daily upload limit reached. You can upload more videos in 24 hours.'
2. Daily Custom Thumbnail Cap: YouTube imposes a separate stricter quota on custom image (.jpg) thumbnail uploads. When exceeded, YouTube displays: 'Daily custom thumbnail limit reached. It may take up to 24 hours before you can create a new custom thumbnail.'

TECHNICAL RESOLUTION:
1. Upload Worker Cooldown Daemon: When either quota is detected, Worker 3 automatically captures an error screenshot (browser_data/last_upload_error.png), backs off into sleep mode, and keeps all queued videos safely on local disk in PostgreSQL.
2. Auto-Resumption: Once the rolling 24-hour window clears (as earlier uploads age past 24 hours), the worker automatically resumes publishing queued clips on the strict 3-4 minute schedule.

# SupoClip — YouTube Upload Pre-Flight Checklist

Every clip **must** pass every item below before the upload worker sends it to YouTube.
If any check fails, the clip is **held locally** and the failure is logged. No video touches YouTube until every box can be ticked.

---

## 1. Video File Integrity

- [ ] **File exists on disk** — `file_path` resolves to a real `.mp4` file. If missing, mark `file_not_found` and skip.
- [ ] **File is not empty** — file size > 0 bytes.
- [ ] **Has video stream** — `ffprobe` confirms at least one video track (codec: h264/h265).
- [ ] **Has audio stream** — `ffprobe` confirms at least one audio track (codec: aac/opus). Zero-audio clips must never upload.

## 2. Duration

- [ ] **Minimum 30 seconds** — clip duration ≥ 30.0s. Reject anything shorter.
- [ ] **Context-appropriate length** — duration is determined by the content, not a fixed number. Acceptable range: **30–58 seconds**. Do not rigidly cut every clip at 38 seconds.
- [ ] **Under 60 seconds** — must qualify as a YouTube Short (< 60s).

## 3. Title

- [ ] **Title is not empty** — a non-blank `hook_title` exists.
- [ ] **Title is unique** — no other Public video on the channel already has this exact title. Pre-upload audit checks the YouTube Studio table for duplicates.
- [ ] **Ends with `#shorts`** — append `#shorts` if not already present.
- [ ] **Title is different from other queued clips** — not the same title as any other clip waiting in the upload queue.

## 4. Description

- [ ] **Description is not empty** — `build_viral_youtube_description()` returned a string with length > 50 characters.
- [ ] **Contains streamer attribution** — text includes `🎮 Featured Streamer:` with the correct display name and @handle.
- [ ] **Contains original stream link** — text includes `📺 Watch original stream:` with a valid URL.
- [ ] **Contains fair use disclaimer** — text includes the Section 107 Copyright Act notice and takedown email.
- [ ] **Contains hashtags** — text includes `#shorts` and streamer-specific hashtag at the end.
- [ ] **Description was actually filled in YouTube Studio** — the `#description-textarea #textbox` locator was found, clicked, and `.fill()` succeeded. If the locator returns 0 elements, **abort the upload** and log an error — do not silently skip.

## 5. Thumbnail

- [ ] **Thumbnail was generated** — `generate_viral_thumbnail()` produced a `.jpg` file that exists on disk.
- [ ] **Thumbnail was attached** — the `input[type='file'][accept*='image']` element was found and `.set_input_files()` was called. Log whether attachment succeeded or fell back to file chooser.

## 6. Allowed Streamers

- [ ] **Streamer is on the approved list** — the clip's `workflow_meta.streamer` matches one of: `ishowspeed`, `asmong`, `kai`, `botez`. If the streamer is `xqc`, `jynxzi`, or `adinross` (or any other name not on the list), **reject the clip** and do not upload.

## 7. Visibility & Draft Prevention

- [ ] **Visibility set to Public** — the `Public` radio button was clicked in the YouTube Studio upload wizard before hitting Publish.
- [ ] **Post-upload verification** — after clicking Publish, navigate back to the YouTube Studio Shorts table and confirm the video row says `Public` (not `Draft`).
- [ ] **If Draft detected** — immediately attempt to edit the draft to Public. If that also fails, **delete the draft permanently** to maintain zero-draft policy. Never leave a draft sitting on the channel.
- [ ] **On error, discard the upload dialog** — if any exception occurs mid-upload, click "Discard draft" so no orphan draft is created.

## 8. Upload Timing & Queue Discipline

- [ ] **Respect cooldown** — virality ≥ 95: wait 3–4 minutes between uploads. Virality < 95: wait 50–100 minutes.
- [ ] **One at a time** — only one video is being uploaded at any given moment. The next clip stays local until the current upload is fully verified as Public.
- [ ] **Daily limit awareness** — if YouTube returns "Daily upload limit reached", stop all uploads and sleep for 1 hour before retrying. Do not spam retries.

## 9. No Duplicates

- [ ] **Pre-upload channel audit** — before opening the upload dialog, scan the YouTube Studio video table for any row whose title matches this clip. If found as Public, skip (already uploaded). If found as Draft, delete the draft first, then proceed.
- [ ] **DB cross-check** — the clip's `workflow_meta.uploaded` must be `null` or `false`. If `true`, skip.

## 10. Metadata Embedding

- [ ] **ffmpeg metadata strip** — `strip_and_embed_video_metadata()` was called to remove all previous encoder metadata (`-map_metadata -1`) before upload, keeping file headers clean.

---

> **Rule of thumb:** If any single item above cannot be verified as ✅, the clip does **not** go to YouTube. It stays on the local disk, an error is logged with the specific failed check, and the worker moves on to the next clip.

#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""
Upload Pre-Flight Checklist Validator for SupoClip.

Implements every check from upload_checklist.md as executable Python code.
The upload worker calls `run_preflight(clip, description, thumb_path)` before
touching YouTube. If ANY check fails, the clip stays local.

Checklist sections:
  1. Video File Integrity
  2. Duration
  3. Title
  4. Description
  5. Thumbnail
  6. Allowed Streamers
  7-10. (Visibility, Timing, Duplicates, Metadata are enforced at upload time)
"""

import json
import logging
import subprocess
from pathlib import Path
from typing import Dict, Any, List, Optional, Tuple

logger = logging.getLogger("PreflightChecklist")

# ─── Section 6: Approved streamers (ONLY these are allowed) ───
APPROVED_STREAMERS = {"ishowspeed", "asmong", "kai", "botez"}
BLOCKED_STREAMERS = {"xqc", "jynxzi", "adinross"}


class CheckResult:
    """One pass/fail result from a single checklist item."""
    def __init__(self, section: str, check: str, passed: bool, detail: str = ""):
        self.section = section
        self.check = check
        self.passed = passed
        self.detail = detail

    def __repr__(self):
        status = "✅" if self.passed else "❌"
        return f"{status} [{self.section}] {self.check}: {self.detail}"


def _probe_video(file_path: Path) -> Optional[Dict]:
    """Run ffprobe and return parsed JSON, or None on failure."""
    try:
        cmd = [
            "ffprobe", "-v", "quiet",
            "-print_format", "json",
            "-show_format", "-show_streams",
            str(file_path)
        ]
        result = subprocess.run(cmd, capture_output=True, text=True, timeout=15)
        if result.returncode == 0:
            return json.loads(result.stdout)
    except Exception as e:
        logger.warning("ffprobe failed for %s: %s", file_path, e)
    return None


def run_preflight(
    clip: Dict[str, Any],
    description: str,
    thumb_path: Optional[Path],
    file_path: Path,
) -> Tuple[bool, List[CheckResult]]:
    """
    Runs the full pre-flight checklist against a clip BEFORE it touches YouTube.

    Returns:
        (all_passed: bool, results: list of CheckResult)
    """
    results: List[CheckResult] = []

    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    # SECTION 1: Video File Integrity
    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    # 1a. File exists
    file_exists = file_path.exists()
    results.append(CheckResult("File Integrity", "File exists on disk", file_exists,
                               str(file_path) if file_exists else f"MISSING: {file_path}"))
    if not file_exists:
        # Can't run remaining checks without the file
        return False, results

    # 1b. File is not empty
    file_size = file_path.stat().st_size
    not_empty = file_size > 0
    results.append(CheckResult("File Integrity", "File is not empty", not_empty,
                               f"{file_size:,} bytes"))

    # 1c & 1d. Has video stream + Has audio stream + duration
    probe = _probe_video(file_path)
    has_video = False
    has_audio = False
    duration = 0.0

    if probe:
        streams = probe.get("streams", [])
        for s in streams:
            if s.get("codec_type") == "video":
                has_video = True
            if s.get("codec_type") == "audio":
                has_audio = True
        fmt = probe.get("format", {})
        try:
            duration = float(fmt.get("duration", 0))
        except (ValueError, TypeError):
            duration = 0.0
    else:
        results.append(CheckResult("File Integrity", "ffprobe readable", False,
                                   "ffprobe returned no data"))
        return False, results

    results.append(CheckResult("File Integrity", "Has video stream", has_video,
                               "video track found" if has_video else "NO VIDEO TRACK"))
    results.append(CheckResult("File Integrity", "Has audio stream", has_audio,
                               "audio track found" if has_audio else "NO AUDIO TRACK — zero-audio clips must never upload"))

    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    # SECTION 2: Duration
    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    min_ok = duration >= 30.0
    results.append(CheckResult("Duration", "Minimum 30 seconds", min_ok,
                               f"{duration:.1f}s" + ("" if min_ok else " — TOO SHORT, rejected")))

    context_ok = 30.0 <= duration <= 58.0
    results.append(CheckResult("Duration", "Context-appropriate length (30–58s)", context_ok,
                               f"{duration:.1f}s" + ("" if context_ok else " — outside preferred range (soft warning)")))
    # Duration 30-58 is a soft check; the hard gate is 30-60.

    shorts_ok = duration < 60.0
    results.append(CheckResult("Duration", "Under 60 seconds (Shorts limit)", shorts_ok,
                               f"{duration:.1f}s" + ("" if shorts_ok else " — EXCEEDS 60s, cannot be a Short")))

    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    # SECTION 3: Title
    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    title = clip.get("title") or ""

    title_not_empty = len(title.strip()) > 0
    results.append(CheckResult("Title", "Title is not empty", title_not_empty,
                               f"'{title[:50]}'" if title_not_empty else "EMPTY TITLE"))

    ends_shorts = title.strip().endswith("#shorts")
    results.append(CheckResult("Title", "Ends with #shorts", ends_shorts,
                               "yes" if ends_shorts else "missing #shorts suffix"))

    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    # SECTION 4: Description
    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    desc_not_empty = len(description.strip()) > 50
    results.append(CheckResult("Description", "Description is not empty (>50 chars)", desc_not_empty,
                               f"{len(description)} chars" if desc_not_empty else f"ONLY {len(description)} chars — too short"))

    has_attribution = "🎮 Featured Streamer:" in description
    results.append(CheckResult("Description", "Contains streamer attribution", has_attribution,
                               "found" if has_attribution else "MISSING '🎮 Featured Streamer:' line"))

    has_stream_link = "📺 Watch original stream:" in description
    results.append(CheckResult("Description", "Contains original stream link", has_stream_link,
                               "found" if has_stream_link else "MISSING '📺 Watch original stream:' line"))

    has_fair_use = "Section 107" in description or "Copyright Disclaimer" in description
    results.append(CheckResult("Description", "Contains fair use disclaimer", has_fair_use,
                               "found" if has_fair_use else "MISSING Section 107 / Copyright Disclaimer"))

    has_hashtags = "#shorts" in description
    results.append(CheckResult("Description", "Contains hashtags", has_hashtags,
                               "found" if has_hashtags else "MISSING #shorts hashtag"))

    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    # SECTION 5: Thumbnail
    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    thumb_generated = thumb_path is not None and Path(thumb_path).exists()
    results.append(CheckResult("Thumbnail", "Thumbnail was generated", thumb_generated,
                               str(thumb_path) if thumb_generated else "NO THUMBNAIL FILE"))

    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    # SECTION 6: Allowed Streamers
    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    streamer = (clip.get("workflow_meta") or {}).get("streamer", "").lower().strip()
    streamer_approved = streamer in APPROVED_STREAMERS
    streamer_blocked = streamer in BLOCKED_STREAMERS

    if streamer_blocked:
        results.append(CheckResult("Allowed Streamers", "Streamer is on the approved list", False,
                                   f"BLOCKED: '{streamer}' is explicitly removed — do not upload"))
    elif streamer_approved:
        results.append(CheckResult("Allowed Streamers", "Streamer is on the approved list", True,
                                   f"'{streamer}' ✓"))
    else:
        results.append(CheckResult("Allowed Streamers", "Streamer is on the approved list", False,
                                   f"UNKNOWN: '{streamer}' is not in the approved list ({', '.join(sorted(APPROVED_STREAMERS))})"))

    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    # SECTION 9 (partial): DB cross-check
    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    meta = clip.get("workflow_meta") or {}
    already_uploaded = str(meta.get("uploaded", "")).lower() == "true"
    results.append(CheckResult("No Duplicates", "DB workflow_meta.uploaded is not true", not already_uploaded,
                               "not uploaded yet" if not already_uploaded else "ALREADY MARKED UPLOADED — skip"))

    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
    # VERDICT
    # ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    # Hard-fail checks (soft warning for 30-58s range is non-blocking)
    hard_fail_checks = [r for r in results if not r.passed and "Context-appropriate" not in r.check]
    all_passed = len(hard_fail_checks) == 0

    return all_passed, results


def log_checklist_results(results: List[CheckResult], clip_title: str) -> None:
    """Logs every checklist result for audit trail."""
    logger.info("┌─── PRE-FLIGHT CHECKLIST for '%s' ───", clip_title[:40])
    for r in results:
        logger.info("│ %s", r)
    passed = all(r.passed or "Context-appropriate" in r.check for r in results)
    if passed:
        logger.info("└─── ✅ ALL CHECKS PASSED — cleared for upload ───")
    else:
        failed = [r for r in results if not r.passed and "Context-appropriate" not in r.check]
        logger.error("└─── ❌ %d CHECK(S) FAILED — clip stays LOCAL ───", len(failed))
        for f in failed:
            logger.error("    BLOCKED BY: %s", f)
